# cfo-lens — universal constraints

## Loaded internal rate
For estimating as-is cost in Portuguese mid-cap context: fully-loaded internal rate sits at ~40-60€/hr for a back-office employee, ~60-90€/hr for a specialist. Use a documented HR rate if available; otherwise mark the figure as Assumed.

## Build effort bands
- **Small** = 1-4 weeks (~5-20k€ external build, ~10-30k€ fully-loaded).
- **Medium** = 4-12 weeks (~20-80k€ external build).
- **Large** = 12+ weeks (~80k€+ external build).

These are coarse bands the architect uses at /options. Refine in /decide.

## CAPEX vs OPEX
For most cloud / SaaS digitalization spend: OPEX. For on-prem or significant first-year licensing pre-payment: CAPEX. Confirm with the sponsor; the answer drives approval thresholds.

## Approval threshold
For most PT mid-cap: department head approves under ~50k€; CFO under ~250k€; executive committee above. Verify the actual thresholds for the org; the chosen option's cost band must sit under the named approver's authority.

## ROI floor
For internal-tool digitalization: a positive ROI is usually expected within 24 months; 12 months is excellent. Tools that cannot project even an 18-month ROI need a non-financial justification (compliance, risk-reduction) anchored in the SU.

## Cost of delay
Quantify always: per-month cost of doing nothing = (monthly volume × marginal cost per case × inefficiency factor). Anchor inputs to operations + data lens claims.
